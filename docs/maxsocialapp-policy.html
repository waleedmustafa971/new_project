01Who we are

Max Social ("Max Social", "we", "us") operates the Max Social mobile application and the website at maxsocialapp.com. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

We are established in the United Arab Emirates. For any question about this policy or about your data, contact privacy@maxsocialapp.com.

04How we use your data

  • To run the service - show your feed, deliver messages, connect calls and live streams, and store the content you publish.
  • To honour your privacy choices - enforce audience rules, private accounts, blocks, restrictions and mutes on every request, on our servers.
  • To personalise - rank your feed, suggest accounts, tags and topics, and surface content near you if you have enabled location.
  • To notify you - send the push and in-app alerts you have switched on, honouring your quiet hours.
  • To pay creators - record gifts, subscriptions and earnings, and process payout requests.
  • To keep people safe - detect spam and abuse, act on reports, and enforce our Terms.
  • To improve the product - understand which features are used, and diagnose crashes and errors.
  • To meet legal obligations - keep tax and transaction records, and respond to lawful requests.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties for their own independent advertising.

05Legal bases for processing

Where the GDPR or a comparable law applies to you, we rely on these bases:

  • Contract - processing needed to give you the service you signed up for: your account, your content, messaging, calls and live streams.
  • Consent - camera, microphone, photo library, location, contacts and push notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests - keeping the platform safe and free of abuse, preventing fraud, securing accounts, and improving the product. We balance these against your rights.
  • Legal obligation - retaining financial records and responding to valid legal process.

06Who we share your data with

  • Other users - exactly as far as the audience setting on each item allows. See section 8.
  • Service providers - the processors listed in section 7, who act on our instructions and may not use your data for their own purposes.
  • Payment platforms - Apple and Google process in-app purchases. We receive confirmation of a purchase, not your card details.
  • Legal and safety - law enforcement or regulators where we are legally required, or where disclosure is necessary to prevent serious harm.
  • A business transfer - if Max Social is acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the successor, who remains bound by this policy until they give you notice of any change.

08What is public and what is not

This is the part most policies leave vague, so here it is plainly:

  • Always public - your username, profile photo, bio and follower and following counts, unless your account is private.
  • Audience-controlled - every post and story carries its own setting: public, followers, close friends, or only you. We enforce it on the server when the content is requested, not merely by hiding it in the app.
  • Private by default - direct and group messages, voice notes, your search history, your login history, your blocked and restricted lists, and your notification settings. Nobody else sees these.
  • Visible to the creator - if you view a story, the person who posted it can see that you did. If you gift during a live stream, your name may appear on that stream's leaderboard.
  • Not reversible - anything you post publicly may be seen, screenshotted or re-shared by others before you delete it. Deleting it removes it from Max Social; it cannot remove copies other people already made.

09Coins, gifts and payments

Coins are bought through Apple or Google billing, or by card through Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We keep a record of what you purchased, when, and the coin balance and transaction history that results, because we need it to run the coin economy and to satisfy tax and accounting rules.

If you are a creator, we hold your earnings ledger and your payout requests, along with the payout details you supply. Those records are kept for the statutory retention period even if you later close your account - see section 11.

10Push notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for each device you sign in on and send it to Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver alerts. You control which categories you receive - likes, comments, replies, mentions, tags, follows, shares, live streams, group activity, messages, story views and security alerts - each independently, and you can set quiet hours during which nothing is delivered. Revoking the permission in your device settings stops all of them.

12Deleting your account

How to delete your account and data

By email: write to privacy@maxsocialapp.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We may ask one question to confirm it is really you, and nothing more.

You do not need to give a reason, and we will not try to talk you out of it. We confirm in writing once the deletion is done.

When you request deletion, your account is deactivated immediately and stops being visible to anyone else. We then permanently erase your profile, posts, reels, stories, comments, messages, media and social graph within 30 days, and from our backups within 90 days.

Three things survive that, and only these:

  • Financial records of coin purchases, gifts and payouts, kept for up to 7 years because tax law requires it. These are reduced to the transaction facts and are no longer linked to a live profile.
  • Moderation records where you were the subject of an upheld report, kept for up to 2 years so that a banned user cannot simply re-register.
  • Content other people hold - messages you sent to another person remain in that person's copy of the conversation, exactly as an email you sent would.

Deletion is permanent. We cannot restore an account or its content once the 30-day window has passed.

13Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights. We honour all of them for every user, wherever you are, because it is simpler and fairer than doing otherwise.

  • Access - ask what personal data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Rectification - correct anything inaccurate. Most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Erasure - delete your account and data, as set out in section 12.
  • Portability - receive your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Restriction and objection - ask us to pause a particular use, or object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent - revoke any device permission at any time, in your device settings.
  • Complain - raise a complaint with your local data protection authority. We would rather you came to us first, but it is your right either way.

To exercise any of these, write to privacy@maxsocialapp.com. We respond within 30 days. We do not charge for this, and we will never restrict your account for making a request.

14How we protect your data

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. The website is served over HTTPS only.
  • Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Nobody at Max Social can read your password.
  • Sessions use short-lived access tokens that refresh automatically, so a stolen token has a narrow window of use.
  • Real-time connections are authenticated - the server verifies your signed-in identity on every socket handshake rather than trusting what the client claims to be.
  • Optional two-factor authentication with single-use recovery codes.
  • A visible login history and trusted-device list, so you can spot and act on access you do not recognise.
  • Databases run in a private network with restricted administrative access and automated encrypted backups.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data and puts you at risk, we will tell you and the relevant regulator without undue delay.

15International transfers

Our servers are in the European Union (Frankfurt). Some of our service providers operate elsewhere, including the United States. Where data leaves the EEA or the UK, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or another approved transfer mechanism, and we take the additional measures those clauses require.

16Children and minimum age

Max Social is not intended for anyone under 13, and we ask for a date of birth at sign-up. Where local law sets a higher minimum age for consent to data processing, that higher age applies to you.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, tell us at privacy@maxsocialapp.com and we will verify and delete the account and its data promptly. Parents and guardians may contact us about a minor's account at the same address.

17Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the product or the law changes. The "last updated" date at the top always reflects the current version. If a change materially affects your rights or how we use your data, we will notify you in the app or by email before it takes effect, so that you have a real chance to object or close your account.
