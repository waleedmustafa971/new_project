Inside the app

Five ways to say something. All in one place.

Most apps make you choose one format and one audience. Max Social gives you the whole range, and lets you decide who sees each thing - per post, per story, per account.

A feed that respects the people in it

Photos, albums and long captions, with hashtags, mentions and location. Every single post carries its own audience setting, so one account can hold your public work and your private life without them ever meeting.

  • ✓ Per-post audience: public, followers, close friends, or only you
  • ✓ Tag review - nothing lands on your profile without your say-so
  • ✓ Hashtags, topic follows and location tagging feed discovery
  • ✓ Saved collections, hidden posts and a mute list that actually sticks

Short video, with the editor built in

Shoot, trim, score and publish without ever leaving the app. Pull a track from the music library, let the AI reel assistant suggest the cut, and post it to the same audience controls as everything else.

  • ✓ In-app capture, trimming and thumbnail selection
  • ✓ AI reel assistant for cuts, captions and pacing
  • ✓ Licensed music library, saved tracks and attribution
  • ✓ Like, comment, save, share - and receive gifts on any reel

Stories that ask, not just tell

Twenty-four hours by default, forever if you want it. Add a poll or a question sticker and read the answers as they arrive. Keep the good ones as highlights on your profile.

  • ✓ Polls, questions and reaction stickers with live results
  • ✓ Highlights - pin the ones worth keeping to your profile
  • ✓ Close friends lists, mentions and swipe-up links
  • ✓ See exactly who viewed, and hide your story from anyone

Conversations that keep up with you

One-to-one and group chat over an authenticated real-time socket. Voice notes, replies, forwarding, emoji reactions, typing indicators and presence - and a call is one tap away from any thread.

  • ✓ Replies, forwarding and message actions in one-to-one and groups
  • ✓ Voice notes recorded and sent inline
  • ✓ Typing indicators, presence and read receipts you can switch off
  • ✓ Every socket connection is verified against your signed-in token

Go live, and bring people on stage

Start a room and invite your audience up beside you. Request a seat, accept or decline, mute, remove, promote a moderator. Viewers send gifts, and the leaderboard shows who is holding the room up.

  • ✓ Multi-guest seats with requests, invites and host approval
  • ✓ Live chat with pinning, deletion, slow mode and moderators
  • ✓ Reactions, gifts and a running gifter leaderboard
  • ✓ Kick, mute and lift controls for the host and their moderators
The full surface

The whole product, one account.

None of this is a roadmap slide. Every capability below is built and running on our servers today.

Posts, albums and the feed

Multi-image posts with captions, hashtags, mentions and place tags. Comments thread with likes and replies, and every post keeps its own audience rule for as long as it exists.

Per-post audienceTag reviewSaved collectionsSoft delete

Reels and the video editor

Vertical video with capture, trim and thumbnail tools in the app, an AI assistant that suggests the cut, and a music library you can save tracks from. Reels take gifts like any other surface.

AI reel assistantIn-app editorMusic libraryGiftable

Stories and highlights

Twenty-four hour stories with poll, question and reaction stickers, swipe-up links, mentions, close-friends targeting and per-viewer visibility. Keep the best as permanent highlights.

Real-time messaging

Direct and group threads over a socket that verifies your token on every handshake. Voice notes, replies, forwarding, reactions, typing indicators and presence.

Voice and video calls

One-to-one HD calling straight from a conversation, with proper incoming-call handling, ringing, decline and in-call controls on both platforms.

Live rooms with people on stage

A live stream is a room, not a broadcast. Guests request a seat or get invited, the host approves, and up to a full stage of co-hosts share audio and video. Chat has moderators, pinning, deletion and mute. Gifts land in real time and the leaderboard keeps score.

Create a group, manage members and roles, and keep a shared conversation with its own posts and media separate from your main feed.

Discovery that goes somewhere

Search people, posts, tags and places with suggestions and trending. Follow hashtags and topics, browse top creators, or look at what is happening near you.

Music

A browsable track library for reels and stories, with saved music on your profile and correct attribution on everything you publish.

Coins, gifts and the store

Buy coins through the app store billing flow, send gifts on reels and in live rooms, and pick up virtual items to equip on your profile.

Live & gifting

The room decides who gets the mic.

A broadcast is one person talking. A Max Social live room is a stage. Viewers ask to come up, the host waves them through, and the conversation belongs to everybody in it.

  • 1Request or invite. Anyone watching can ask for a seat. The host can also pull someone up directly, and either side can decline without drama.
  • 2Moderate in the open. Promote moderators, pin a message, delete one, mute a guest, kick someone out and lift it later. Every action is logged.
  • 3Gifts turn into earnings. Coins sent during a stream land on the leaderboard instantly and roll into the creator earnings ledger behind it.

Privacy & safety

The controls are not buried three menus deep.

Privacy is not a settings page we added at the end. It runs through the data model - every post, story and profile carries its own audience, and every one of these controls is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

Reporting with follow-upReport a post, comment, account or stream - then check the status of what you filed.

Two-factor sign-inAuthenticator-based 2FA with single-use recovery codes kept for you.
